Linking Back to Primary Goals

There are three easy ways that you can link back to Primary Goals to qualify for your free listing on the referral page.

1. Direct Linking From Your Own Site

You can copy the link or text below and place it somewhere on your website.

Example: Ashley Guberman, Primary Goals Consulting
HTML: <a href="http://www.PrimaryGoals.com">Ashley Guberman, Primary Goals Consulting</a>

Put the URL from your site where the link can be found into your profile.

2. Linking From Your Blog

If you have a blog site, you can place the link above on one of your blog pages.
Put the URL to your blog article in your profile, then I will enable your entry on the reference pages.

3. Social Bookmarking

If you have an account with any of the social bookmarking sites, you may add a bookmark to my home page directly from here.
Mouse-over or click on the image below, then select the bookmarking service that you use.
